FAQ

Good to know

Can anyone book a seat?

Yes. Solo travellers, couples, families and groups can all request seats. Tell us how many you need in the booking form.

What's the difference between Luxury and Heritage?

Luxury Charter focuses on comfort, refreshments and a relaxed pace. Heritage & Culture focuses on guided storytelling about the railway, its stations and the hill communities. Not sure? Pick "Not sure" and we'll help.

Is my booking confirmed when I submit the form?

Submitting sends a seat request, not a confirmed ticket. We'll reach out to confirm availability, timings and pricing for your date.

When is the best time to visit?

Spring and autumn usually bring clearer skies and the best chance of Himalayan views. The monsoon months make the hills lush and misty, with views that can come and go.

Where do journeys start?

Journeys are planned from Siliguri, West Bengal. We'll share exact boarding details when we confirm your seats.
